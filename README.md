### 📈 [Chart Vault](https://chart-vault-mu.vercel.app)
**A free workspace for chart-driven traders** · *Web · Live*

Forex, crypto and US stock traders keep their watched pairs and chart images in one place, analyze on their own charts and get notified when price hits their levels. Multi-user and completely free.

- 🖊️ **Own charting**: TradingView-style drawing tools (Fibonacci, long/short, channels, brush), templates, magnet, 30+ themes
- 🔔 **Price alerts** via email & Telegram, checked every 20 s (Binance + my own MT5 bridge)
- ⏪ **Bar replay** for manual backtesting with orders, SL/TP and partial closes
- 🗂️ **Watchlists & chart archive** (lossless WebP), US stocks, world market sessions
- 📱 Installable PWA with a dedicated mobile layout

### 📓 [EdgeBook](https://github.com/YasirCelikStack/edgebook)
**A trading journal with an AI coach** · *Desktop · Windows*

A dark, trader-terminal style desktop app that turns your trade history into answers: what works, what doesn't, and which habits cost you money.

- 📊 **Dashboard, calendar & pivot analytics**: P&L by session, setup, weekday, tag; export to Excel
- 🤖 **AI coach** (Google Gemini): reads your stats and finds hidden behavioral patterns
- 🛡️ **Risk rules** per account: daily loss limit and max drawdown meters
- 🕐 **Session detection**: New York time, ICT killzones
- 🔗 **Chart Vault sync**: imports journal entries *and* their chart screenshots
- 🧱 **Layered OOP architecture**: UI → Services → Data → Core, generic repositories, EF Core TPH inheritance
